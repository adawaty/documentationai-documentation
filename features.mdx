## Overview

Tawplast provides comprehensive plastic injection molding services tailored for OEMs and brands. You benefit from our `80-650 t` press range, diverse material support, continuous `24/7` production, engineering Design for Manufacturability (DFM) assistance, and finishing options. These capabilities ensure high-quality parts shipped directly to your specifications.

<Callout kind="info">
  All capabilities support EU, GCC, and Africa markets with in-house tooling and ship-direct quality control.
</Callout>

## Press Range and Capacity

Our presses handle clamping forces from `80 t` to `650 t`, accommodating small precision parts to large components. This range supports high-volume production runs.

| Press Size | Shot Size (g) | Applications |
|------------|---------------|--------------|
| `80-150 t` | `50-250`     | Small parts, toys, stationery |
| `200-350 t` | `200-600`    | Automotive components, sprayers |
| `400-650 t` | `500-1500`   | Appliance housings, large assemblies |

## Supported Materials

Tawplast molds a variety of engineering plastics optimized for your industry needs.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Commodity" icon="package">
    **PP (Polypropylene)**: Cost-effective for containers and toys. High impact resistance.
    
    **ABS**: Tough and versatile for automotive and consumer goods.

    ```json
    {
      "material": "PP",
      "properties": {
        "tensileStrength": "30 MPa",
        "density": "0.90 g/cm³"
      }
    }
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Engineering" icon="settings">
    **PC (Polycarbonate)**: Transparent, impact-resistant for lenses and enclosures.
    
    **PA6-GF (Nylon 6 Glass-Filled)**: Strong for structural parts with `30%` glass fiber reinforcement.

    ```json
    {
      "material": "PA6-GF30",
      "properties": {
        "tensileStrength": "140 MPa",
        "heatDeflection": "215°C"
      }
    }
    ```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
