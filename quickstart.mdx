## Prerequisites

<Callout kind="info">

Before starting, gather these essentials:

- A design drawing in STEP, IGES, or DXF format
- Expected annual quantity (`>10,000` units recommended)
- Preferred material (e.g., PP, ABS, PS)
- Delivery location (EU, GCC, Africa supported)

</Callout>

## Step-by-Step Quote Request

Follow these steps to submit your first quote request.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare Your Design Drawing" icon="file-text">
    Export your CAD model to a compatible format. Ensure tolerances are specified and drafts are at least `1.5°`.

    ```bash
    # Example export from SolidWorks
    File → Save As → STEP AP214 (*.step)
    ```

    <Image
      src="https://tawplast.com/assets/sprayer-Ci7gyI3c.png"
      alt="Sample agricultural sprayer part molded by Tawplast"
      width="400"
      height="300"
    />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Gather Required Information" icon="list">
    Compile details for accurate quoting.

    <Columns cols={2}>
      <Card title="Part Details" icon="package">
        - Dimensions and weight
        - Annual volume
        - Color requirements
      </Card>
      <Card title="Additional Specs" icon="settings">
        - Material grade
        - Surface finish (textured, polished)
        - Packaging needs
      </Card>
    </Columns>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit Your Request" icon="send">
    Use the contact form or email. Upload files directly.

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Web Form" icon="globe">
        Visit [https://tawplast.com/contact](https://tawplast.com/contact).

        1. Select "Request a Quote"
        2. Upload drawing (`<50MB`)
        3. Fill in part details
        4. Submit
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Email" icon="mail">
        Send to sales@tawplast.com.

````markdown
Subject: Quote Request - [Your Part Name]

Attachments: drawing.step, specs.pdf

Message body:
Part: [Description]
Quantity: [e.g., 50,000/year]
Material: [e.g., ABS]
```
````
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Expect Our Response" icon="clock">
    We review submissions Monday-Friday.

    <Callout kind="tip">
      Typical response: `24-48 hours`. Complex tools may take `3-5 days`.
    </Callout>

    Track status via reply email. Next: tooling discussion and sample run.
  </Step>
</Steps>
