---
title: Test Samples Not Received
description: What to do if your test submission is missing.
---

## Symptoms

- Dashboard says "Awaiting Samples"
- No progress after shipping

## Causes

- Shipment delayed
- Label not scanned

## Solution

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check tracking">
    Get your shipment's tracking link and confirm delivery status online.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact support">
    Email tracking info to support@mlminasaat.com if status hasn't updated within 2 business days.
  </Step>
</Steps>
