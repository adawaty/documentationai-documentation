## Overview

You need to create a test order in your dashboard and ship samples to our lab.

## Steps

1. Log in to your ML Minasaat dashboard
2. Go to "Testing" > "New Sample Submission"
3. Fill in part details and select required tests
4. Download the shipment label
5. Ship your physical samples to:
   - ML Minasaat Lab
   - 123 Plastic Park, Suite 10
6. Check dashboard for status updates
