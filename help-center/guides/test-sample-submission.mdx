## Overview

Easily test your part using our portal.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account">
    Sign up at https://dashboard.mlminasaat.com
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new test order">
    Go to "Testing" > "New Sample Submission" and fill out the form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pack and ship part">
    Use the generated label and send the part to our address. Include order number inside the package.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track progress">
    Check your dashboard for lab status and results.
  </Step>
</Steps>
